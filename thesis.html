The category that doesn't exist yet

A century ago, physical fitness wasn't a category. The idea that an ordinary person would train their body every day had to be invented. Then it became normal.

Mental fitness is where physical fitness was then. We have a system for mental health, for when something is wrong. We have almost nothing for the far larger group who aren't ill, but aren't at their best either.

That empty space is the category. Not care for the few, but training for the many.

Why it couldn't exist until now

Everyone has always known what works: the right practice, often enough, fitted to the person and the day. The problem was never the knowledge. It was delivery.

Depth meant a human: precise, but an hour a week, and only for some. Apps and books reached millions, but gave everyone the same thing.

Deep but scarce, or scalable but generic. Nothing could be both. For the first time, that constraint is gone. The science was always there. The delivery wasn't.
