Recognition

Some of this will sound familiar.

Most of what weighs people down isn't illness. It's an everyday mind carrying more than it was ever trained to carry. None of this is strange, and none of it is just you.

Your head won't switch off at night, replaying the same thoughts.

Learn to see a thought as a thought instead of a fact, so it loses its grip.

One setback knocks you off course for days.

Train to recover faster and find your footing sooner.

You snap under pressure, then regret how you reacted.

Build a small pause between what happens and how you respond.

You keep worrying about things you can't control.

Practice letting a feeling be there without letting it run your day.

You're harder on yourself than you'd ever be on a friend.

Learn to treat yourself with the same fairness you give others.

Your attention is everywhere, pulled away the moment you focus.

Train to bring it back, on purpose.

What surprises most people is how small the starting point is. Not willpower, not positive thinking. A few minutes a day, on the thing that fits you right now. This is how every human mind works, and like everyone's, yours can be trained.
